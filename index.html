Manifesto

No Computers.
No Driver Aids.
Just Modena Steel.

Born from an uncompromising refusal to dilute mechanical feedback. Every control cable, gear tooth, and intake trumpet directly connects driver to machine.

Origin

The 1988 Homologation.

Conceived in an independent Modena atelier to challenge the era's rising weight and synthetic driver aids. The GT-88 distilled the pure wedge silhouette into its rawest, most visceral road incarnation.

Craft — 01

Thermal Architecture.

Ten precision matte-black louvers evacuate heat directly from the mid-mounted engine bay. Negative pressure at speed extracts four hundred cubic feet of air per minute.

Craft — 02

Retractable Pods.

Flush-fit motorized pods preserve zero-drag laminar flow during daylight. At dusk, dual electro-mechanical servos deploy twin quad-projector lamps in 0.4 seconds.
